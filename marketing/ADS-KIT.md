## Compliance

- Landing page has no cookie and no tracker: adding a conversion tag requires a consent banner (CNIL) — decide with the owner first.
- Claims in ads must be literally true on the landing page (Google Ads misrepresentation policy).
- Trademarks of other products (Claude, Codex, Cursor…) only in a descriptive way, never as the ad's brand.
